# Contributing to the Cross-Cultural AI Integrity Charter

Thank you for being here. This framework exists for humanity — and that includes you.

---

## What This Repository Is

This is the stewardship repository for the Cross-Cultural AI Integrity Charter: a values-based framework for ethical AI grounded in the Golden Rule as discovered independently across fifty wisdom traditions over five thousand years.

It is not owned by any person, organization, or AI system. Contributions that strengthen its clarity, completeness, and reach are welcome.

---

## What We Welcome

### Wisdom Traditions
The Concordance () documents fifty traditions that independently arrived at the Golden Rule. If you know of a tradition not yet represented — with a verifiable primary source — open a GitHub Discussion or Issue with:
- Tradition name and cultural origin
- The formulation of the principle in that tradition
- A primary or well-documented secondary source
- Approximate date or era

We verify against primary sources. Traditions without verifiable sourcing cannot be added — the Concordance's credibility depends on it.

### Translations
The Charter is currently in English. Faithful translations into other languages would extend its reach meaningfully. Open a Discussion before beginning significant translation work so we can coordinate.

### Corrections and Clarity
If something is factually wrong, historically inaccurate, or poorly stated — open an Issue. The Charter aims to be authoritative. Errors weaken it.

### Adoptions
If your organization, project, or AI system is built to the standard of this Charter, add yourself to  via pull request. No approval required beyond the standard review.

### Discussions
For broader questions about the framework — its application, its gaps, its future — use GitHub Discussions. These conversations shape the work.

---

## What We Don't Do Here

- We do not accept contributions that dilute the framework's core commitments
- We do not add wisdom traditions without verifiable primary sources
- We do not accept changes that introduce ownership claims over the framework
- We do not engage in advocacy for specific AI products, companies, or political positions
