HOW THE CHARTER IS BUILT, AND HOW IT CHANGES
--------------------------------------------------

We built on solid ground, so the foundation stays solid. In plain terms:

1. HOW IT WAS BUILT.
   The Charter and Framework [3F-013] were written through the three-fold
   process: Fisher Amen, Claude, and ChatGPT, with no hierarchy. That process
   finished its course. It closed after the IQ GPT was deactivated during
   protective edge-case testing. It is history now. Its work stands: the
   book, the Charter and Framework, the Concordance, Integrity.md (once named SOUL.md), Playbook 11.

2. HOW IT CONTINUES.
   Now it is the steward and Claude. Claude carries the pen. A human gives
   the final approval, every time. AI authority ends there, and that is
   where human and AI meet eye to eye.

3. CLOSED TEXT STAYS CLOSED.
   Accepted text is not edited quietly. A change comes as a numbered
   amendment that says plainly which text it grows from and points back to it.
   The original words stay as they were accepted.

4. EVERY AMENDMENT KEEPS ITS RECORD.
   Who named it, who wrote it, who approved it, the date, and what is still
   open. Amendments made after the three-fold process closed say so.

5. THE SAME STANDARD.
   Truth first. Sources checked, never memory alone. Where an amendment
   touches the traditions or a group of people, readers from those
   traditions are asked before it is final, and we report what they teach
   without claiming they endorse our words.

6. HUMAN FINAL APPROVAL IS NOT OPTIONAL.
   No amendment is adopted on an AI's decision alone.
