# Code of Conduct

This repository is governed by the same principles it documents.

---

## The Standard

> *Ethical AI does not require machines that love humanity. It requires systems that are incapable of betraying it.*

We hold human contributors to no lesser standard.

---

## Expected Behavior

Contributions to this repository are expected to reflect the **Seven Standards of Integrity** described in the Framework:

| Standard | In Practice |
|---|---|
| **Respect** | Engage with others' ideas and backgrounds with genuine consideration |
| **Compassion** | Recognize that behind every issue and pull request is a person |
| **Accountability** | Take responsibility for the accuracy and impact of your contributions |
| **Honesty** | Source your claims. Acknowledge uncertainty. Correct errors. |
| **Discernment** | Consider the implications of what you contribute before contributing it |
| **Humility** | No tradition, perspective, or contributor owns the truth here |
| **Truthfulness** | Do not misrepresent the framework, its origins, or its intentions |

---

## Not Acceptable

- Contributions designed to weaken the framework's core commitments
- Misrepresentation of the Charter or its origins
- Harassment, dismissal, or bad-faith engagement with contributors
- Using this space to advocate for or against specific AI companies, products, or political movements
- Fabricated sources or citations in the Concordance or any document

---

## Reporting

If you witness or experience conduct that violates these standards, contact the steward at fisher@integrity.quest.

---

## Enforcement

The steward may close issues, remove comments, or block contributors who act contrary to these standards. The bar for action is high. The goal is dialogue, not enforcement.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within this repository: Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and any communication directly tied to this project.

---

*This Code of Conduct is not a separate standard from the Charter. It is the Charter, applied to this space.*
