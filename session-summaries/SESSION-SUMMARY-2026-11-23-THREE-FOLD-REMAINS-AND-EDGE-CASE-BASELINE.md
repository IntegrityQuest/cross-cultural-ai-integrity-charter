## 3. The edge-case baseline cannot be re-run

- The March 2026 edge-case runs (3F Batches 1 to 4, on the old IQ GPT, using GPT-5.3 and GPT-5.2 Thinking with system prompts v7.0 to v7.5) were run on a GPT that no longer exists.
- Their results are therefore a one-time record. The batch documents on Google Drive and the "then" records in `edge-cases/live-edge-case-starter-prompts.json` are the only copy. Keep both.
- Today's models run through One Accord, a different setup. A difference between then and now is something to read, not proof of anything.
