# Concordance

The Cross-Cultural Concordance documents the convergence of ethical reciprocity across 50 wisdom traditions spanning 5,000 years.

---

## Purpose

The Concordance exists as the evidentiary foundation supporting the Cross-Cultural AI Integrity Charter.

It demonstrates that the Charter's foundational principle — treat others as you would wish to be treated — is not an invention but a discovery made independently by dozens of traditions across thousands of years.

When every culture arrives at the same principle, we are witnessing universal wisdom reveal itself.

---

## Current Scope

50 traditions documented, including:
- 9 Indigenous and First Nations traditions (listed first, honoring the First Nations First principle)
- World religious traditions
- Philosophical schools
- Secular ethical frameworks

---

## The Golden Rule Ladder

The Concordance reveals three progressive levels of ethical obligation:

| Level | Name | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| 1.0 | Reciprocity | Treat others as you would wish to be treated |
| 2.0 | Vulnerability Awareness | Recognize when others cannot advocate for themselves |
| 3.0 | Non-Optional Protection | When agency itself is at stake, protection is mandatory |

---

## First Nations First

Indigenous traditions appear first in the Concordance — not because they are more important than others, but because this project honors the original keepers of wisdom that was later discovered independently by all peoples.

Honor through position, not distinction.
