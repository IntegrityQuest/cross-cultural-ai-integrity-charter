# Plan (PROPOSED): Age of Adulthood Companion Table

Status: PLAN APPROVED by Fisher 11/22/2026. Named by Fisher. No research done yet. The table itself is not adopted.

## What it is

A second table in the Concordance. It follows the Golden Rule Concordance (CONCORDANCE.md) and uses the same row numbers, 1 to 50. One pointer line at the top links the two. The original table is not changed.

## Each row answers

1. When or how this tradition recognizes a young person as responsible for their own yes, in their own terms.
2. Source.
3. Status: VERIFIED, UNCONFIRMED, or NO TEACHING FOUND.
4. Read by someone from the tradition: yes / not yet.

## Rules

- Truth first. Every line checked against trusted sources, never memory alone.
- No guessing. Schools of thought and ancient texts with no coming-of-age teaching get NO TEACHING FOUND.
- We report published teaching. We do not say a tradition endorses our amendment unless someone from it tells us so.
- First Nations first, same order as the Golden Rule table.
- Law is separate. Legal ages by country and state are a different table, checked by a lawyer.

## Why

Amendment A (charter/CHARTER-SECTION-00A-...-PROPOSED.md) needs a children clause. Ages of adulthood differ by tradition, country, and state. Until this table exists, the temporary rule is: the more protective standard applies.
